## Introduction

The Explorer shows you what is inside your knowledge: which sources are connected, which documents each one holds, and whether those documents are ready to be found by your agents. You can open a document, read the original file, and check where an answer came from.

## Where to find it

Open **Knowledge hub** in the **Build** section of the left menu. It is shown to admins and builders. It lists the sources of the space. Click a source to open its page.

## The sources hub

Tabs sort the sources: **Business context**, **Application knowledge**, and **Shared with this space** for sources that another space shares with yours. For each source you see its name, type, access, freshness, size, whether it is active, status, connection and last update. Use **Search knowledge…** to filter the list.

At the top, a **Source health** summary counts documents, partial runs, overdue sources, failed documents and documents without chunks. It also lists the sources to review. Click one to open its page on the right tab.

A source status is one of **Ready**, **Indexing**, **Error**, **Cancelled**, **Unknown** or **Never run**.

## A source page

A source page has five tabs: **Overview**, **Documents** (or **Data**), **Activity**, **Retrieval** and **Settings**. A source shared by another space opens in read-only view, without these tabs.

- **Connection health** shows the success rate, the last sync, the average latency and the errors of the last 7 days.
- **Activity** lists the runs of the source with their status and time. Open a run to see its start, end, duration and error, and to retry a failed or cancelled run.
- **Documents** holds the funnel and the list of documents.

### The document funnel

The funnel shows how far your documents get:

1. **Listed**: every document the source knows.
2. **Ready**: documents that were processed.
3. **Searchable**: ready documents that your agents can actually find.

Between two steps, you see what was lost, for example documents that are **Failed**, **Skipped** or **Queued**, or documents **without chunks**. Click a step or a loss to filter the list on it. **Searchable** and **without chunks** are not filters.

## The list of documents

Search by name with **Search documents…**, and filter by **Status**. On an upload or Drive source, you can also filter by **Family**: **Upload** or **Drive file**.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Queued** | Waiting to be processed |
| **Processing** | Being processed now |
| **Ready** | Processed (searchable once it has chunks) |
| **Failed** | Could not be processed |
| **Skipped** | Not read |
| **Stale** | Its index was cleared; the next run of the source indexes it again |

The list updates by itself while documents are being processed.

## Open a document

Click a document to open its drawer. It has three tabs:

- **Details**: type, family, status, last update and last indexed date.
- **Preview**: the original file.
- **Extracted text**: the text that Scalt found in the file, split into passages. What AI added, the context of a passage and the questions about the document, is marked **AI-generated**.

From the drawer you can download the file, or open it where it comes from (**Open in Google Drive** or **Open page**).

### The original file

The **Preview** tab shows the original:

- **PDF** and **images**: shown as they are. A PDF that is too large to preview can be downloaded.
- **Office files** (Word and PowerPoint, and similar formats): they can be converted to a PDF for preview. When a preview is available, you see **Preparing the preview…** the first time you open the file, then the file. The layout is recalculated, so pages may differ from the original: download the file for the exact layout. When no preview is available (**Preview isn't available for this file type.**), download the file to read it.
- Other types: no preview, but you can still download the file.

## Citations

When citations are shown in an answer, click a citation to open its source in a side drawer. For a PDF it opens on the cited page, and for a presentation on the cited slide. You see the **Cited passage**, or the **Cited extract** kept with the answer if you cannot browse the source. A note tells you if **the document changed since the answer**.

This also works for a file attached to a conversation or a case.

You can open the original of a cited document even if you cannot browse its source. The extracted text of the document stays reserved to people who can browse the source.

## Search with ⌘K

Press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows) anywhere in Scalt. The search covers **Pages**, **Docs**, **Cases**, **Sources**, **Documents** and **Passages** of all your spaces, your private space included. Type at least two characters. A passage opens its document at the right place.

## Who sees what

- Everyone who opens the Knowledge hub sees the sources of the space.
- Only an admin of the space and the person who created a source can browse its documents. The **Source health** counts follow the same rule.
- Another person's private sources are never shown, not even to admins.
- If you cannot browse a source, you see **Documents are restricted**.

## Stop a run

When a run is in progress, the owner of the source or an admin of its space can click **Stop run** on the source page. For an organization-wide source, only an organization admin can. Every run in progress stops: syncs, scheduled runs, re-embeds and uploads.

- A re-embed stopped halfway leaves a partial index. Re-embed the source again.
- A stopped upload leaves its document to process again.

## Delete a document

Open the document and click **Delete…**. You can delete uploads, Drive files, pages, articles and web pages, if you may edit the source. For an organization-wide source, only an organization admin can.

A deleted document disappears from search and from the list. The copy that Scalt stored of it is erased as well, except for a web page, whose archived copy is kept for now. A document that was synced from another tool comes back only if it changes at its origin.

## Known limits

- A document is searchable only once it is **Ready**. A **Ready** document without chunks cannot be found: check the funnel.
- When Office previews are available, they are rebuilt pages: they can differ from the original. Download the file to be sure.
- After a deletion, allow a short moment for the erasure to finish everywhere.
- The Explorer lists the documents stored in the space you are in. Documents stored in another space do not appear.
