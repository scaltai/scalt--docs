## Introduction

The Explorer shows you what is inside your knowledge: which sources are connected, which documents each one holds, and whether those documents are ready to be found by your agents. You can open a document, read the original file, and check where an answer came from.

<Info>
  The Explorer is available if it is activated for your space.
</Info>

## Where to find it

Open **Knowledge hub** in the left menu. It lists your sources. Click a source to open its page.

## The sources hub

The hub groups your sources by what they hold, such as **Business context** and **Application knowledge**. For each source you see its name, type, access, freshness, size, status and last update. Use **Search knowledge…** to filter the list.

At the top, a **Source health** summary counts your documents, partial runs, overdue sources, failed documents and documents without chunks. It also lists the sources to review. Click one to open its page on the right tab.

A source status is one of **Ready**, **Indexing**, **Error**, **Cancelled**, **Unknown** or **Never run**.

## A source page

A source page has five tabs: **Overview**, **Documents** (or **Data**), **Activity**, **Retrieval** and **Settings**.

- **Connection health** shows the success rate, the last sync, the average latency and the errors of the last 7 days.
- **Activity** lists the runs of the source, with their status, start, end and duration. Open a run to see its error.
- **Documents** holds the funnel and the list of documents.

### The document funnel

The funnel shows how far your documents get:

1. **Listed**: every document the source knows.
2. **Ready**: documents that were processed.
3. **Searchable**: ready documents that your agents can actually find.

Between two steps, you see what was lost, for example documents that are **Failed**, **Skipped** or **Queued**, or documents **without chunks**. Click a number to filter the list on it.

## The list of documents

Search by name with **Search documents…**, and filter by **Status**. For sources that mix several kinds of files, you can also filter by **Family**: **Upload**, **Drive file**, **Page**, **Article** or **Web page**.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Queued** | Waiting to be processed |
| **Processing** | Being processed now |
| **Ready** | Processed and searchable |
| **Failed** | Could not be processed |
| **Skipped** | Not read |
| **Stale** | Replaced by a newer version |

The list updates by itself while documents are being processed.

## Open a document

Click a document to open its drawer. It has three tabs:

- **Details**: family, status, last update and last indexed date.
- **Preview**: the original file.
- **Extracted text**: the text that Scalt found in the file, split into passages. Passages written by AI are marked **AI-generated**.

From the drawer you can download the file, or open it where it comes from (**Open in Google Drive** or **Open page**).

### The original file

The **Preview** tab shows the original:

- **PDF** and **images**: shown as they are. A PDF that is too large to preview can be downloaded.
- **Office files** (Word and PowerPoint, and similar formats): converted to a PDF the first time you open them. You see **Preparing the preview…**, then the file. The layout is recalculated, so pages may differ from the original. Download the file for the exact layout.
- Other types: no preview, but you can still download the file.

## Citations

When an answer quotes one of your documents, click the citation. The source opens in a side drawer, on the cited page when the file has pages. You see the **Cited extract**, and a note if **the document changed since the answer**.

This also works for a file attached to a conversation or a case.

You can open the original of a cited document even if you cannot browse its source. The extracted text of the document stays reserved to people who can browse the source.

## Search with ⌘K

Press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows) anywhere in Scalt. The search covers **Pages**, **Docs**, **Cases**, **Sources**, **Documents** and **Passages** of your space. Type at least two characters. A passage opens its document at the right place.

## Who sees what

- An **admin of the space** sees the sources shared with the space, and their own sources.
- **Everyone else** sees only the sources they created.
- Another person's private sources are never shown, not even to admins.
- If you cannot browse a source, you see **Documents are restricted**.

## Stop a run

On a source page, an admin of the space or the owner of the source can click **Stop run**. Every run in progress stops: syncs, scheduled runs, re-embeds and uploads.

- A re-embed stopped halfway leaves a partial index. Re-embed the source again.
- A stopped upload leaves its document to process again.

## Delete a document

Open the document and click **Delete…**. You can delete uploads, Drive files, pages, articles and web pages, if you may edit the source.

A deleted document disappears from search and from the list. The copy that Scalt stored of it is erased as well, except for a web page, whose archived copy is kept for now. A document that was synced from another tool comes back only if it changes at its origin.

## Known limits

- A document is searchable only once it is **Ready**. A **Ready** document without chunks cannot be found: check the funnel.
- Office previews are rebuilt pages: they can differ from the original. Download the file to be sure.
- After a deletion, allow a short moment for the erasure to finish everywhere.
- The Explorer lists the documents stored in the space you are in. Documents stored in another space do not appear.
